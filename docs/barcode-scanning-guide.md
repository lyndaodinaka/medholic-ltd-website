# Medholic Pharmacy Barcode Scanning Guide

Medholic Pharmacy can work with phones, laptops, desktops, and barcode scanners.

## Best Options

1. **USB barcode scanner on laptop or desktop**
   - Best for pharmacy counter sales.
   - Plug the scanner into the computer.
   - Click the barcode or stock-code field.
   - Scan the product.
   - The barcode appears like typed text.

2. **Laptop or desktop webcam**
   - Good if the computer has a camera.
   - Open **Capture** and click **Start camera**.
   - Hold the barcode clearly in front of the webcam.
   - Works best with good lighting.

3. **Phone camera**
   - Best for moving around shelves.
   - Useful for stock checks, expiry checks, and shelf inventory.
   - Open the app on the phone and use **Start camera**.

4. **Manual entry**
   - Type the barcode or product code directly into the barcode/stock-code field.
   - Useful when the barcode is damaged or the scanner is not available.

5. **Generate Stock Code**
   - Use this when a medicine, supply, or equipment item has no barcode.
   - The app creates a unique code such as `MED-YYMMDD-XXXX`.
   - Write or print that code on the shelf label or product record.

## Recommended Pharmacy Setup

- Use a laptop or desktop at the counter.
- Connect a USB barcode scanner for fast sales.
- Keep phone camera scanning for shelf movement and inventory checks.
- Use generated stock codes for items without barcodes, such as some supplies, devices, or locally packed items.

## Important Note

Generated stock codes identify the item inside Medholic Pharmacy. They do not automatically know the medicine name unless the item has already been saved with that code in inventory.
