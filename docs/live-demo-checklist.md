# Medholic Pharmacy Live Demo Checklist

Use this when showing Medholic to a pharmacy owner, clinic, buyer, or investor.

## Before The Demo

- Open the live Medholic app.
- Confirm manager login works.
- Keep private login details hidden.
- Prepare one sample medicine.
- Prepare one sample sale.
- Open the proposal page in another tab.

## Demo Flow

1. Show the Medholic Pharmacy logo and clean login page.
2. Explain that staff can log in with roles.
3. Open Dashboard and show stock, sales, low-stock, and expiry summaries.
4. Open Capture and show barcode/manual/generated stock code entry.
5. Select a medication and show function, recommended dose guidance, and side effects.
6. Show quantity received, quantity sold, and how many are left.
7. Open Sales and record a sample sale.
8. Show controlled medicine doctor report warning.
9. Open Inventory and show low-stock and expiry alerts.
10. Open Reports and show downloadable inventory/security reports.
11. Open Security and show audit logs, cash checks, stock adjustments, and lead requests.
12. Show the proposal page and explain Starter, Standard, and Premium.

## Questions To Ask

- How do you currently track stock?
- How often do you check expiry?
- Do you need staff sales tracking?
- Do you need supplier reorder support?
- How many branches do you have?
- Do you need setup and training?

## After The Demo

- Set the lead status to Demo booked or Quoted.
- Send the follow-up email template.
- Prepare a quote if they are serious.
- If they agree, move the lead to Won and use the onboarding checklist.
