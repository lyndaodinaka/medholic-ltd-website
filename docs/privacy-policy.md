# Medholic Pharmacy Privacy Policy Template

Medholic Pharmacy records inventory, sales, suppliers, staff activity, and audit events for pharmacy operations.

## Data The App May Store

- Medicine and product names.
- Stock quantities.
- Supplier company names, phone numbers, and email addresses.
- Staff names and roles.
- Sales records.
- Cash drawer checks.
- Stock adjustment records.
- Audit history.
- Doctor report or prescription reference for controlled-medicine sales.

## Local Prototype Storage

This prototype stores data in the browser. A production system should store data in a secure backend database with access controls, backups, and audit protection.

## Recommended Production Controls

- HTTPS.
- Staff permissions.
- Password reset.
- Data backups.
- Audit log retention.
- Controlled access to reports.
