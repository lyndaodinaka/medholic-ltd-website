# Medholic Pharmacy Customer Sales Script

## Simple Opening Message

Hello, my name is Lynda from Medholic Pharmacy. I built a pharmacy management app that helps pharmacies track stock, sales, expiry dates, suppliers, staff activity, reports, and missing-product risks from one dashboard.

## Main Benefits To Mention

- Know how many medicines are left.
- See expiry warnings before products are wasted.
- Track who sold what.
- Save supplier phone and email.
- Download inventory and security reports.
- Reduce missing money or product confusion.

## Questions To Ask A Pharmacy

1. Do you currently track medicines by book, Excel, or software?
2. How do you know when stock is getting low?
3. Do you check medicines expiring within 90 days?
4. Do you need staff sales tracking?
5. Do you want setup and training included?

## Closing Message

I can arrange a demo and recommend the best Medholic plan for your pharmacy size. For enquiries, contact lynda.chidi@medholic.net.

## Offer Structure

- Starter: one pharmacy, stock and sales tracking.
- Standard: reports, staff roles, suppliers, and security tracing.
- Premium: branch setup, training, branding, and priority support.
