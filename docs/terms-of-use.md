# Medholic Pharmacy Terms Of Use Starter

Medholic Pharmacy is intended to support pharmacy inventory, sales, supplier, reporting, and audit workflows.

## Clinical Safety

Medicine functions, recommended-dose notes, and side-effect notes must be checked against the product label, prescription, doctor report, or pharmacy-approved protocol before use.

## Controlled Medicines

Controlled/opioid medicines must only be supplied according to applicable law, pharmacy policy, and valid doctor report or prescription requirements.

## Prototype Limitation

This browser-based version is a prototype. Do not rely on it as the only source of truth for live pharmacy operations until a secure backend database and access-control system is added.
