IP / licensing opportunity

Spotit Wound App

Wound management software for capture, assessment, review visibility and clearer wound documentation. Available for demo, licensing, partnership or acquisition conversations.

Open details
For sale / licensing Buy the concept, IP, workflow and documentation. Complete final clinical, safety, testing and deployment assurance under your own governance.

Designed for quick wound-care clarity

Spotit is designed around a simple flow: capture the wound, assess risk, compare progress, document reviews and support team decisions from one clear view.

  • Wound image capture and structured observation records.
  • Visible wound signs, including exudate, flagged for clinician review.
  • Previous and present wound comparison for progress conversations.
  • Demo-ready presentation for buyers, reviewers and pilot partners.

Current positioning: Spotit is being sold or licensed from its current stage. Medholic Ltd is selling/licensing the app concept, IP, workflow and supporting documents, not taking responsibility for final clinical deployment compliance.
