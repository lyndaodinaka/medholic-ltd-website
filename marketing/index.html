Featured project

Spotit Wound App

Spotit is a wound-management project created from nursing insight to help clinicians capture wounds, track progress, review changes, and present wound care information more clearly.

Wound-management workflow

A nurse captures a wound securely on a mobile device, visible observations such as exudate can be flagged for clinician review, and the care team can compare previous and present wound progress for better review conversations.

  • Capture wound images and structured observations in one place.
  • Flag visible signs such as purulent exudate for clinician review.
  • Compare previous and current wound records to support treatment planning.
  • Prepare clearer summaries for doctors, tissue viability teams, pilot partners, and service leads.

Current positioning: private demo, buyer review, and controlled testing while clinical safety, governance, information governance, and organisation approvals are completed.
