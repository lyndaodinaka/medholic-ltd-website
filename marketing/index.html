Spotit brings wound management into a clearer digital careflow.

Spotit is a Medholic wound-management project created from nursing insight to help clinicians capture wounds, track progress, review changes, and present wound care information more clearly.

Spotit Wound App

Designed for wound documentation, review planning, caseload visibility, and clinician-to-clinician communication.

Request a Spotit demo See the workflow

Spotit in action

A lively wound-management journey from capture to clinical review.

Picture a nurse in a professional UK clinical uniform supporting a patient with calm, holistic care. The wound is captured securely on a mobile device, the record highlights visible wound observations such as exudate, and the care team can compare previous and present wound progress for better review conversations.

  • Capture wound images and structured observations in one place.
  • Flag visible signs such as purulent exudate for clinician review.
  • Compare previous and current wound records to support treatment planning.
  • Prepare clearer summaries for doctors, tissue viability teams, pilot partners, and service leads.

Spotit is positioned for private demo, buyer review, and controlled testing while clinical safety, governance, information governance, and organisation approvals are completed.
