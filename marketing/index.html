Featured project Spotit Wound App Click to see what it is about

Spotit is a wound-care app developed from nursing experience to support clinicians with wound documentation, review planning, caseload visibility, and care workflow.

  • Helps organise wound records, observations, and review notes.
  • Supports clinicians with clearer follow-up planning and care visibility.
  • Designed around real careflow pressure, documentation needs, and team communication.
  • Useful for presenting a structured wound-care workflow to healthcare teams, reviewers, or pilot partners.

Current positioning: private demo, buyer review, and controlled testing until the right healthcare governance, clinical safety, and organisation approvals are complete.
