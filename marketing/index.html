Featured project

Spotit Wound App

A wound-management app concept created from nursing insight to support wound capture, structured observations, progress review, and clearer clinical communication.

Simple clinical workflow

Spotit is designed around a clear journey: capture the wound, record key observations, compare progress, and support review conversations between nurses, doctors, tissue viability teams, and project partners.

  • Wound image capture and structured observation records.
  • Visible wound signs, including exudate, flagged for clinician review.
  • Previous and present wound comparison for progress conversations.
  • Demo-ready presentation for buyers, reviewers, and pilot partners.

Current positioning: private demo, buyer review, and controlled testing while clinical safety, governance, information governance, and organisation approvals are completed.
