Wound-care technology

A clearer way to capture, monitor and document wound progress.

Spotit is a digital wound-care concept that helps care teams keep wound photos, notes, review status and progress information in one clearer place.

Request a private demo See how it helps

How it helps

Three simple jobs.

1

Capture

Take wound photos and keep them with the right notes, dates and care information.

2

Monitor

Compare previous and current wound records so changes are easier to see.

3

Document

Support clearer reviews, handovers, reports and care-team communication.

Why it matters

Wound information should be easy to find and easy to understand.

When wound photos, notes and review dates are scattered, teams can lose time. Spotit is designed to make wound progress easier to follow, so the right conversations can happen sooner.
