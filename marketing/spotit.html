Spotit product page

Wound management software IP for digital wound assessment and wound documentation.

Spotit is a Medholic Ltd health-tech project created to support clearer wound image capture, structured wound documentation, digital wound assessment, review prompts, and progress comparison for care homes, clinical teams, tissue viability services and healthcare buyers.

Acquisition opportunity

Buy or license Spotit from its current development stage.

Medholic Ltd is selling or licensing the Spotit app idea, intellectual property, workflow, documentation and supporting evidence references. It is suitable for a buyer, partner, care-home technology provider, clinical software team, wound-care business or healthcare investor who wants to continue development and complete final assurance under their own governance.

Available

IP, workflow and documentation

Spotit includes wound-management workflow thinking, app concept, documentation structure, evidence pack references and buyer demo access.

Buyer responsibility

Final safety and deployment

The buyer or deploying organisation remains responsible for CSO appointment, clinical safety approval, functionality testing, MHRA/regulatory assessment where applicable, pilot sign-off and live deployment compliance.

Evidence pack

Prepared references for buyer due diligence.

Evidence references include the Spotit DCB0129 Hazard Log Draft, clinical safety case report, PDS FHIR Healthcare Worker Access Risk Log, IAR/ROPA register, DSPT standards met certificate, and service and incident management process.

Important boundary:

Privacy notice, DPIA, penetration testing summary, pilot report and customer SOPs should be completed, replaced or owned by the buyer before live clinical deployment.
