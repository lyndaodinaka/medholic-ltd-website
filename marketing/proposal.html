Medholic Pharmacy

A modern pharmacy operations app for stock control, sales tracking, expiry alerts, supplier ordering, staff activity, and security reports.

Built to help pharmacy owners know what is left, what is selling, what is expiring, and who handled each action.

Problem

Many pharmacies still depend on notebooks, scattered spreadsheets, memory, or manual stock checks. This can make it hard to trace missing products, expiry losses, supplier orders, and seller accountability.

Solution

Medholic Pharmacy gives pharmacy teams a focused dashboard for inventory, sales, expiry dates, suppliers, reports, controlled medicine warnings, staff roles, and audit records.
