# Medholic Pharmacy

![Medholic Pharmacy logo](../assets/medholic-pharmacy-logo-transparent.png)

## Pharmacy Inventory, Sales, Supplier, And Security Tracking

Medholic Pharmacy helps pharmacies capture medicines and supplies, track stock, record sales, monitor expiry dates, manage suppliers, and detect possible cash or product loss.

## Key Features

- Barcode scanning or generated stock codes.
- Medication and supply catalogue.
- Adult and children medicines.
- Controlled-medicine sale warning and doctor report reference.
- Supplier email and phone storage.
- Low-stock and expiry alerts.
- Sales and gain reports.
- Cash drawer checks.
- Stock count variance checks.
- Audit trail for traceability.
- Downloadable inventory and security reports.

## Target Users

- Independent pharmacies.
- Hospital pharmacies.
- Small clinics.
- Medicine stores.
- Inventory and cashier teams.

## Brand Message

Friendly, modern, and practical pharmacy operations software for safer inventory and better accountability.
